# ⚡ Quick Start — First block in 30 minutes

> Our goal: get you from **unboxing to running your first program in 30 minutes**. Designed so you can do it alone, even if this is your first time.

---

## 📋 Overall Flow

```
① Unbox              (3 min)
② Install app & connect  (5 min)
③ Run your first block   (5 min)
④ Try a sensor           (5 min)
⑤ Firmware transformation (10 min · optional)
```

---

## 🛠 What you need

- ELIO board (in the box)
- Smartphone (iOS 14+ or Android 7+)
- USB-C charging cable
- (Optional) LEGO / DC / servo motor for motion testing

---

## 1️⃣ Unbox

👉 [**Unboxing Guide**](/en/quickstart/unboxing/)

---

## 2️⃣ Install App & Connect

Choose your platform:

- 📱 [**Android**](/en/quickstart/android/)
- 🍎 [**iOS**](/en/quickstart/ios/)

---

## 3️⃣ First Block — Spin a Motor

In the app's **Control** tab, move a slider to spin DC1 motor.

---

## 4️⃣ Try a Sensor

Connect an ultrasonic sensor (5V + IO1 + IO2). In the app's **Sensors** tab, enable **Ultrasonic ON** and wave your hand — watch the distance value change.

---

## 5️⃣ Firmware Transformation (optional)

ELIO has **4 personalities**. Swap profiles from the mobile app.

👉 [**One board, four personalities**](/en/firmware/)
