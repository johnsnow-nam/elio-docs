## ⚡ How to Switch (30 seconds)

```
① Open app  →  ② Firmware menu  →  ③ Tap desired profile  →  ④ DFU runs (30s–1min)
```

- Profile replaces without erasing your data
- Recommended: 30%+ battery before switching
- Keep USB power connected during the update

---

## 🧠 Why Multi-Profile?

Competitors: one board = one use case — buy multiple boards for different projects.  
**ELIO: one board = four use cases. Buy once, get four robots.**
