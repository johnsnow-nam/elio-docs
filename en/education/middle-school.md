# 🚗 Future eco-friendly EV (Middle school Tech class)

> **Audience**: Middle school Technology & Home Economics (recommended: Grade 9 / 3rd year)  
> **Format**: Team project (3 students per team)  
> **Duration**: **4 sessions** (core) + optional extension (club/free-semester)  
> **Assessment**: 25 points (individual worksheet 10 + team work 15)

![Project theme](/elio-docs/assets/downloads/edu/future-transport-01.png)

---

## Summary

Students design and build a cardboard model of a **future eco-friendly vehicle**, mount **ELIO** and **two DC motors**, then drive it with a smartphone and complete missions.

## Teacher prep (per team)

- ELIO board (charged)
- 2× DC motors (wheel + motor set)
- A3 corrugated cardboard × 3, ruler, cutter/scissors, glue gun, straws, velcro
- 1 smartphone per team with the control app (**bletoy**)

## Extensions (after the core 4 sessions)

- More smartphone missions: soccer, sumo, fastest car, servo steering, robot arm  
- PC coding missions: obstacle avoidance (ultrasonic), line tracer (IR), maze escape (Entry/Scratch/Python)
