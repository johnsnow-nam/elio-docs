# Upcycle recyclables into your own car (with ELIO)

> This page is an **English summary** of a six-session Korean elementary (Grade 6) *Practical Arts* unit: **reuse → recycling → upcycling**, then build a **remote-controlled car** from **waste materials + ELIO**.  
> For the full detail (tables, activity sheets, and Korean school terminology), see the [**Korean version**](/elio-docs/education/elementary-upcycling-vehicle/).

---

## Unit at a glance

| Session | Focus |
|---------|--------|
| 1 | **Reuse vs recycling** — value, methods, difference |
| 2 | **Upcycling** — definition, examples, vs simple recycling |
| 3 | **ELIO** — board, app (bletoy), model editor, drive a demo vehicle |
| 4–5 | **Build** a body from **waste** (bottles, foam, etc.), mount motors, connect to ELIO, tune the phone controller |
| 6 | **Race**, measure course length, discuss **speed** (distance/time), reflect |

---

## What teachers need

- One ELIO board (or set) per team, **smartphones** with the control app, **scissors/tape/glue gun** (supervised), **waste** craft materials, small **DC motors** and wheels as in your kit, **9V** or kit-appropriate power per lesson plan.  
- Up-to-date wiring and app steps: [Quick start](/elio-docs/quickstart/), [Motor guide](/elio-docs/connectors/motor-guide/).

---

## Safety

- **Glue gun** and **blades** only with teacher supervision.  
- Keep fingers clear of **spinning motor** shafts.  
- Match **battery and polarity** to your current ELIO hardware manual.
