# Line Following Robot — From Scratch to Autonomous

**Platform**: Instructables  
**Status**: 🔲 Frame only (Wave 1) — full content in Wave 2  
**Target audience**: Intermediate makers, STEM educators  
**Est. read time**: 12 minutes  
**Est. build time**: 1 hour

---

## Introduction (Outline)

> Draw a line. ELIO follows it. Using the dual line sensors in elio-sensor firmware, your robot traces any black-on-white path you create.

**Key hook**: "The oldest robotics demo, reinvented. Draw your track on paper — ELIO runs it."

**What we'll build**: A line-following bot that completes a taped course on the floor.

---

## Supplies (Outline)

- ELIO board with elio-sensor firmware
- Line sensors × 2 (TCRT5000 or compatible)
- Black electrical tape (track material)
- White paper/foam board (track surface)
- Chassis + DC motors

---

## Steps (Outline)

1. Understanding line sensors (reflectance)
2. Mounting sensors on chassis (forward-facing, ~5mm from floor)
3. Calibration: black vs. white threshold
4. Simple 2-sensor logic (left/right/center)
5. Entry blocks: sensor → decision → motor
6. Python version with PID control
7. Challenge: timed course run
