# WiFi Robot with ESP32-CAM + ELIO — See What Your Robot Sees

**Platform**: Instructables  
**Status**: 🔲 Frame only (Wave 1) — full content in Wave 2  
**Target audience**: Intermediate to advanced makers  
**Est. read time**: 15 minutes  
**Est. build time**: 2 hours

---

## Introduction (Outline)

> ESP32-CAM becomes ELIO's WiFi-connected eyes and brain. Stream live video, control motors over HTTP, and trigger AI inference — all on a $10 module.

**Key hook**: "Your smartphone streams the camera. Your browser drives the robot. AI decides when to stop."

**What we'll build**: A WiFi FPV (first-person-view) robot car with live camera stream and HTTP motor control.

---

## Supplies (Outline)

- ESP32-CAM (AI-Thinker)
- ELIO board (Gen3)
- 3 jumper wires (TX/RX/GND)
- Arduino IDE + ESP32 board package
- Your WiFi network

---

## Steps (Outline)

1. ESP32-CAM hardware overview
2. Wiring ESP32-CAM ↔ ELIO UART
3. Flashing ELIOCameraWebServer firmware
4. Connecting to WiFi and finding IP
5. Accessing the web control panel
6. Driving the robot from browser
7. Enabling AI inference (object detection)
