# Straw Frame Robot — Build Any Shape, Make It Move

**Platform**: Instructables  
**Status**: 🔲 Frame only (Wave 1) — full content in Wave 2  
**Target audience**: Educators, kids (ages 8+), maker spaces  
**Est. read time**: 10 minutes  
**Est. build time**: 45 minutes

---

## Introduction (Outline)

> Straws + clips + ELIO = infinite robot shapes. This tutorial introduces the straw frame system — a safe, cheap, creative chassis for any ELIO project.

**Key hook**: "You don't need a 3D printer or laser cutter. A bag of straws and your imagination is enough."

**Why straws?**
- Safe (no sharp edges)
- Cheap (pennies per piece)
- Lightweight
- Configurable: triangles for rigidity, squares for flexibility

**What we'll build**: A straw-frame RC car — chosen shape, student's choice chassis.

---

## Supplies (Outline)

- Plastic straws (straight, ~20 per robot)
- Straw connectors / clips (ball joints or elbow connectors)
- ELIO board
- Hobby motors with wheels
- Rubber bands or hot glue (for securing motors)
- Double-sided tape (for mounting ELIO)

---

## Steps (Outline)

1. Introduction to straw frame geometry (triangle vs. square)
2. Building a rectangular car chassis
3. Adding axle mounts for wheels
4. Securing ELIO board to chassis
5. Connecting motors with cable (audio jack or RJ45)
6. App control test
7. Challenge cards: 60 robot shapes (see Education section)
