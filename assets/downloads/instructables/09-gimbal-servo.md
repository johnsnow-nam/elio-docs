# 2-Axis Camera Gimbal with elio-servo Firmware

**Platform**: Instructables  
**Status**: 🔲 Frame only (Wave 1) — full content in Wave 2  
**Target audience**: Photography enthusiasts, drone builders  
**Est. read time**: 10 minutes  
**Est. build time**: 1.5 hours

---

## Introduction (Outline)

> Switch ELIO to elio-servo firmware, attach two micro servos, and you have a smartphone-controlled 2-axis gimbal for your camera or phone. Smooth pan + tilt, instantly.

**Key hook**: "One firmware update transforms ELIO from a car controller into a camera gimbal. No hardware change needed."

**What we'll build**: A 3D-printed (or cardboard) 2-axis gimbal controlled by the bletoy app's gyroscope mode — tilt your phone, tilt the camera.

---

## Supplies (Outline)

- ELIO board with elio-servo firmware
- Micro servo × 2 (SG90 or MG90S)
- 3D-printed gimbal bracket (files linked) or cardboard build
- Smartphone with bletoy app
- Small camera or phone to mount

---

## Steps (Outline)

1. Flash elio-servo firmware
2. Servo wiring to SV1 / SV2 ports
3. Build or print gimbal bracket
4. Mount servos and camera
5. App gyroscope mode: phone tilt → servo angle
6. Calibration: center position, max angle
7. Advanced: Pixhawk TELEM2 connection for drone gimbal
