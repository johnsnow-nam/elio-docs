# Lego Motors Meet ELIO — Bring Your Bricks to Life

**Platform**: Instructables  
**Status**: 🔲 Frame only (Wave 1) — full content in Wave 2  
**Target audience**: Lego fans, parents, educators  
**Est. read time**: 12 minutes  
**Est. build time**: 45 minutes

---

## Introduction (Outline)

> ELIO has a secret: it speaks Lego. With the Lego Power Functions gender adapter included in every ELIO kit, your existing Lego motors plug right in — no modification, no soldering.

**Key hook**: "You've built Lego sets for years. This tutorial teaches your bricks to obey your smartphone."

**What we'll build**: A Lego Technic chassis with ELIO brain, controlled by the bletoy app.

---

## Supplies (Outline)

- ELIO board
- Lego Power Functions motors × 2 (or Lego Technic set with motors)
- Lego Technic chassis pieces
- ELIO Lego gender adapter (included in kit)
- Smartphone + bletoy app

---

## Steps (Outline)

1. Introduction to Lego Power Functions connector
2. Attaching the ELIO gender adapter
3. Building a Lego chassis
4. Mounting ELIO on the chassis
5. Connecting motors
6. Powering on and connecting via app
7. Driving and calibrating

---

## Why This Tutorial Matters

Lego Technic motors are high-quality, geared, and reliable. ELIO gives them a BLE-connected smartphone brain without replacing the Lego ecosystem.
